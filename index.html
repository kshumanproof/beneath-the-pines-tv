Soperton, Georgia · Pine Sap Road · Harris Land

The World

A family empire rooted in blood.

In Treutlen County, Georgia, the pine forests run deep. So do the grudges. Harris Timber was built on land, silence, and the kind of family history people bury because naming it would mean answering for it.

When Harlan Harris dies, his estranged son Gray is dragged back to Soperton — back to the timber company, back to Pine Sap Road, and back to the names carved into trees like grave markers nobody was supposed to find.

This is not a ghost story. It’s worse. It’s inheritance.

The Tone

Ozark married Succession. Their baby was named Yellowstone. True Detective raised it in the woods.

A prestige Southern Gothic crime mystery about wealth, land, legacy, corruption, old violence, and the terrifying suspicion that some families do not escape their sins — they pass them down.

The Land

The Family

The Secret
