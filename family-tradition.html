The Short

One family. One table. No way out.

Family Tradition pulls the larger series into one contained pressure cooker: a family cornered by old violence, bad blood, and a truth that should have stayed buried.

It is designed to show the audience what the series feels like before a single episode is made: Southern Gothic atmosphere, criminal inheritance, family rot, and the dread of realizing the past is still alive.

Tone

Southern Gothic pressure.

Rain, bloodline, silence, and the feeling that the woods know more than the people do.
